FAQ

Before you reserve

Do I need padel experience?

No. We group players by level on day one, from first-timers to tournament players.

How big are the groups?

Each group is capped at 10 players. Everyone gets real attention from the coaches, and tours and dinners stay intimate.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. Choose a single room, or we can pair you with another player.

Do I need a visa for Georgia?

Citizens of the EU, UK, US and many other countries can enter visa-free. Check the rules for your passport before booking.

What isn't included?

Flights, lunches, dinners outside the listed supras, and travel insurance.

How does reserving work?

Send a request and we confirm dates and price within 24 hours. A deposit secures your place.

Do you host company teams?

Yes. Private dates, an internal tournament and meeting space can be arranged.
